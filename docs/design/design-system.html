The Vidya design system.

A small, opinionated set of primitives. Six colour roles. One type pair. Four border radii. Hand this to engineering and they should be able to build any new screen without inventing a single value.

01Principles

Five rules that override everything below. If a design choice fights one of these, the choice is wrong.

01

Earned ink

Every element earns its place. Whitespace is content. Avoid filler stats, decorative icons, gradients-for-show.

DO · Cut three rows that say nothing.
DON'T · Add a fourth chart to "balance" the page.
02

Colour means something

Subject hues identify subjects. Status hues identify state. Never reuse them across roles.

DO · Chemistry uses amber-400 (#BA7517). Weak status uses amber-600 (#854F0B).
DON'T · Decorate a card with a random subject hue.
03

Numbers are first-class

Scores, XP, days, percentages — set in JetBrains Mono, tabular, slightly tighter tracking. They scan as data, not prose.

DO · 78% in mono with -0.02em letter-spacing.
DON'T · 78% in Inter regular among prose.
04

Hairlines, not shadows

Surfaces separate via 0.5px borders, never elevation. Shadows are reserved for focus rings.

DO · 0.5px solid var(--color-border-subtle).
DON'T · Drop-shadow a card to "lift" it.
05

Motion confirms, never decorates

150ms colour for hover/focus. 500ms spring only on progress fills. No mount-in animations.

DO · Confirm a tap with 100ms scale(0.98).
DON'T · Slide cards in on page load.
06

India-mobile-first

Designed for 390px, 4G, one-handed reach, mid-range Android. Hit targets ≥ 44px. Type ≥ 13px in body.

DO · 44px tap targets, generous spacing on bottom nav.
DON'T · Cram 6-tab nav with 11px labels.

07Motion

Five durations, four eases. Most things should be instant or 150ms. The spring is reserved for one place: progress fills.

--d-mod200msdecelDrawer open, expand
--d-slow300msdecelPage transitions

10Breakpoints

Student and parent journeys are designed mobile-first at 390px, then scaled up. Admin is desktop-first at 1280px. Tablet is graceful degradation only.
