Stuck on Q4 of physics practice. Why is the answer 2 m/s² and not 0.5?
Good question. F = m × a. So a = F/m. You had F=10N, m=5kg.
a = 10 ÷ 5 = 2 m/s²
If you got 0.5, you probably divided 5/10 instead of 10/5. Easy flip — happens a lot.
Ohhh. So mass goes on the bottom always.
Exactly. Force = mass × acceleration, so to find acceleration you isolate it: a = F / m. Mass is always the divisor.
